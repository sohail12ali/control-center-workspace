- Concurrent claim conflict (8-way, different agents): exactly one succeeds — `test_concurrent_claims_by_different_agents_exactly_one_succeeds`, re-run 3x, stable.
- Concurrent `atomic_update` (4-thread increment): no lost updates — `TestAtomicUpdate`.
